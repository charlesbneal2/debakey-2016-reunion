Privacy

This site sells tickets to the DeBakey Class of 2016 ten-year reunion. It is run by classmates, not by the school or district.

What we collect

Your name, your guest's name if you give one, your email, and whether you checked in at the door.

Stripe handles your card. We never see or store your card number. Stripe's privacy policy applies to your payment.

How we use it

We use it to email your tickets, check you in, and contact you about the reunion. Your email goes through our email provider, Resend, and our data is stored with Supabase. We don't sell your information.

What's public

Your name appears under "Who's coming" only if you check that box, and it shows whether you're bringing a guest. Your guest's name and your email are never public.

Refunds and removal

To request a refund, take your name off the list, or have your information deleted, email info@debakey2016reunion.com.

We plan to delete ticket records after the reunion.
